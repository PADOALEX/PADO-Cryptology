# Audit of Python mathematical modules

The complete `tab6.py` through `tab10.py` sources in the supplied archive were read before C# implementation. `tools/reference_vectors.py` extracts pure original methods with Python AST (without importing tkinter), seeds the original PRNG, and records source hashes and reproducible examples in `tests/vectors/mathematics.json`. These are educational compatibility checks, not an endorsement of the original cryptography.

| Module | Correct mathematics | Defects / unsafe behavior | Duplication | Migration and checks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tab6.py` | Recursive Bézout recurrence; positive-modulus square-and-multiply for positive exponents; Miller–Rabin decomposition and witness loop. | GCD sign is not normalized; `a=1,b=0` makes inverse display divide by zero. Exponent zero modulo one returns 1 rather than 0. `random` is predictable. Prime search may miss an existing prime after 1000 samples. Five MR rounds are a probability claim, not a proof. Synchronous loops block UI. | Its extended GCD and MR recur in tabs 8, 9, 10. | Central iterative `BigInteger` arithmetic, nonnegative GCD and sign-correct coefficients; positive inverse modulus >1; exponent ≥0 and modulus >0; CSPRNG rejection sampling; explicit positive rounds and cancellation. | `tab7.py` | Jacobi factors of two, reciprocity and terminal GCD test are correct for positive odd n, including Jacobi(a,1)=1. SS compares Euler power against the Jacobi residue correctly. | Zero or negative iteration count accepts every odd candidate ≥5. Predictable `random`; probable-prime UI result may overstate certainty. No cancellation. | Modular exponentiation uses Python builtin while tab6 reimplements it. | Shared Jacobi and arithmetic with domain checks; SS rounds must be ≥1; CSPRNG uniform witnesses. Tests include negative numerator, non-coprime input, n=1, invalid even/negative n, composites/Carmichael numbers, prime and rounds rejection. |
| `tab8.py` | For distinct primes, n=pq, phi=(p−1)(q−1), coprime e and d=e⁻¹ mod phi are correct. | Manual p=q is accepted although phi formula is then wrong. e=65537 can be ≥phi. Predictable PRNG. | Again copies GCD/MR; inverse is a thin duplicated wrapper. | Require 1≤k≤n<p, 0≤secret<p, validated prime in both operations. Tests reconstruct every threshold subset, import/export and malformed metadata/point cases. |

Shamir does **not** authenticate shares. Exactly k valid-looking shares always interpolate to some polynomial; arbitrary tampering cannot generally be detected with only those k shares. Additional shares can expose inconsistency but do not identify an attacker or replace authentication. Field, threshold and total-share metadata are validated structure, not an authenticity guarantee. Nonzero leading-coefficient conditioning slightly changes the textbook independent-uniform-coefficient distribution; it is used here to satisfy the educational requirement of exact degree k−1 and is not claimed to be a hardened secret-management protocol.

All educational number-theory operations are variable-time and may expose intermediate values in requested traces. Do not use them for confidential production key operations.
